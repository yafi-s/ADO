# Research and asset credits

Crosswalk Lab studies whether a learned driver can follow short Town01 routes, steer through junctions and respond to staged pedestrians and lead vehicles. The simulator supplies the route and ego localization. Driving outcomes apply to the staged encounters, one CARLA town and one vehicle model. [Dosovitskiy et al., CARLA (2017)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v78/dosovitskiy17a.html)

## Demonstrations and learned control

CARLA's built-in Traffic Manager drives the ego car during data collection. Each row pairs observations from one simulation frame with the steering, throttle and brake applied on the next tick. Pomerleau's ALVINN is an early example of supervised steering, and Chen et al. motivate collecting demonstrations from a teacher with access to simulator state. In this experiment, the learner sees other road users through its sensor inputs. Scenario scripts use actor state to stage the encounters, and the evaluator uses it to measure them. [Pomerleau, ALVINN (1988)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/1988/file/812b4ba287f5ee0bc9d43bbf5bbe87fb-Paper.pdf) · [Chen et al., Learning by Cheating (2020)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v100/chen20a.html) · [CARLA Traffic Manager](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/adv_traffic_manager/)

Two small multilayer perceptrons learn the actuator commands. The steering network receives ego speed and six coordinates describing three upcoming route points at 3, 7 and 13 m. The points come from the provided map route and perfect simulator pose and are expressed in the ego car's forward and right axes. The junction scenarios stage left and right turns with a green signal. Codevilla et al. motivate conditioning a learned driver on navigation. The pedal network receives ego speed and perception features and learns signed demand from demonstrated throttle minus brake. Positive demand applies throttle, negative demand applies brake, and zero releases both. The learned commands drive the stock CARLA Lincoln MKZ 2020. [Codevilla et al., End-to-End Driving via Conditional Imitation Learning (2018)](https://vladlen.info/publications/conditional-imitation-learning/)

## Perception and the sensor ablation

Both policies use the forward RGB camera. The frozen, pretrained NanoDet model contributes confidence, bounding-box height and horizontal position for a central pedestrian and a central vehicle when detected. RGB patch statistics summarize colors and intensity variation in the region ahead. The fusion policy adds lidar and Doppler radar features such as forward clearance, return counts and radial velocity. Both policies use the same demonstration rows, steering inputs and training procedure. The comparison tests the effect of adding lidar and radar to a policy that already uses RGB and speed. [NanoDet project](https://github.com/RangiLyu/nanodet) · [Pretrained weights and license](../vendor/nanodet/README.md) · [CARLA sensor models](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/ref_sensors/)

Waymo's public sixth-generation hardware description inspired the use of overlapping camera, lidar and radar coverage. Crosswalk Lab uses one simulated front RGB camera, a rotating lidar and a forward radar, with coverage and error characteristics set by those CARLA sensor models. [Waymo sixth-generation hardware](https://waymo.com/blog/2024/08/meet-the-6th-generation-waymo-driver/)

## Closed-loop evaluation

The driver makes a decision every 0.1 seconds of simulated time. NanoDet runs on alternate ticks at 5 Hz, with its previous detections reused in between. The RGB patch and range features update on the 10 Hz control loop. Synchronous fixed-step execution aligns observations and controls to simulator frames. Matched seeds pair route, encounter and starting-pose settings across policies. During a drive, a policy's actions affect its next observations and can change when it encounters a staged actor. CARLA documents extra conditions for deterministic reruns, including reloading the world for each repetition. [CARLA synchronization and time step](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/adv_synchrony_timestep/#physics-determinism)

Eight seed values (11–18) supply demonstrations for the clear-road, pedestrian crossing, stopped-lead and turn scenarios. Four held-out seed values (101–104) are used for closed-loop tests. Both demonstrations and tests vary initial lateral offset and heading using the scenario seed. The tests also include a lead vehicle that brakes sharply, an encounter absent from the demonstration set. Route completion, contacts, lateral error and minimum hazard gap describe what happened over a drive. Per-frame actuator error supplies a separate diagnostic. A policy can match many demonstration frames and still fail to recover after a mistake, a known limitation of behavior cloning. [Codevilla et al., Exploring the Limitations of Behavior Cloning for Autonomous Driving (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08980)

## Tire traction and the infrared view

The driving study uses stock vehicle physics. A separate straight-line braking experiment commands full braking from approximately 8 m/s at three CARLA tire-friction settings, 1×, 0.5× and 0.25× the stock value. It verifies the wheel setting and measures the route distance to the first sample at or below 0.15 m/s. The settings are simulator parameters. Wet-looking CARLA weather presets describe rendered appearance, and the traction experiment changes wheel friction explicitly. [CARLA vehicle physics](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/tuto_G_control_vehicle_physics/) · [CARLA wheel implementation](https://github.com/carla-simulator/carla/blob/0.9.16/Unreal/CarlaUE4/Plugins/Carla/Source/Carla/Vehicle/CarlaWheeledVehicle.cpp) · [CARLA weather parameters](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/python_api/#carlaweatherparameters)

The chase camera and modeled long-wave infrared channel are observer views. The infrared renderer combines semantic material assumptions and aligned depth with emission, reflection and atmospheric attenuation in the 8–14 µm band. The result is an uncalibrated simulation for visualization. [Radiance model and assumptions](thermal.md) · [CARLA semantic camera](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.16/ref_sensors/#semantic-segmentation-camera)
