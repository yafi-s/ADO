## Matched unoccupied boulevard

Both models drove the same 285.49 m Town03 lane from rest, using three native cameras, LiDAR, radar, vehicle dynamics and CARLA's posted speed. The world contained no other vehicles or walkers. Native speed limits went from 30 to 90, 60 and 30 km/h. Both runs finished without contact or a 40 km/h overshoot. The predeclared interior interval runs from 60.0 to 273.49 m and includes only frames on which the posted limit permits 40.

The original v0.3 driver reached a median 28.42 km/h in that interval. The revised checkpoint reached a median 39.58 km/h, with standard deviation falling from 3.37 to 1.75 km/h and mean absolute speed change decreasing from 1.95 to 1.03 km/h per second. Time commanding more than 0.05 brake decreased from 2.2 to 0.7 seconds. The seven remaining braking frames followed a peripheral front-camera person classification. No dynamic walker was present, and corridor and side range measurements remained clear. The revised driver first sustained 38–40 km/h at 9.0 seconds, held that range for 11.5 continuous seconds and spent 16.0 seconds in it overall. The original driver peaked at 36.61 km/h. [Original Low run](boulevard/city-cruise/speeds.json) · [Revised Low run before sign preview](boulevard/city-pace/speeds.json) · [Matched fixture and scoring](../../crosswalk/city_boulevard.py)

The final transition exposed a missing planning input. The Low run requested 30 km/h on the first frame in the new zone, but its actual speed was still 39.97 km/h. It spent 1.3 seconds above the lower limit plus 1 km/h. Town03 OpenDRIVE supplies a roadside `Speed_30` object at road station 284.3 on the negative-lane carriageway. The route preview projects same-direction signs onto the driven lane and uses a comfortable approach envelope. Applying this preview to all 3,351 recorded seed-211 observations changed no planned speed requests. The empty approach needed a correction about 29 m before the native limit changed. ### Signed approach on the High-rendering host

The same revised checkpoint drove the unoccupied boulevard twice on the same High-rendering server with the sign preview disabled and enabled. In the control it entered the 30 km/h zone at 39.35 km/h. With preview it began governing 31.35 m before the limit changed, entered at 30.16 km/h and peaked 0.38 km/h above the lower limit. Time above the posted limit plus 1 km/h dropped from 1.3 s to zero. The 90 to 60 km/h transition imposed no speed correction on a car already travelling below 40.

With preview, the interior median was 38.68 km/h and the peak was 40.036 km/h, including 0.2 s just above 40. The longest uninterrupted 35–40 km/h interval lasted 10.3 s, and the longest 38–40 interval lasted 7.3 s. Intentional braking for the 30 sign increases dispersion in the full window. On just the 90 km/h portion of the interior, standard deviation was 0.66 km/h with preview and 0.64 without. Both cars remained in lane and finished without contact. ## Reproduction

The model and its fit use the archived seed-75 instructor episode. These different starts remain development tests on a known simulator layout. Speed measurements use CARLA velocity every 0.1 seconds. Instantaneous acceleration uses consecutive samples and half-second estimates use differences over five ticks. [Model and Colab reproduction](../../models/city-pace/README.md) · [Speed and comfort calculation](../../tools/measure_city_cruise.py)

```sh
python -m crosswalk.city_drive --model models/city-pace/candidate.npz --seed 211 --tactical --no-surround --output out/pace-check
python tools/measure_city_cruise.py results/city-pace/seed-211
python -m crosswalk.city_pace_challenges --output out/pace-challenges

```
