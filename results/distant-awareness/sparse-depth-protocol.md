# Sparse pedestrian depth study

The 100 m lidar’s angular sampling leaves some small pedestrian boxes with only one or two exclusive returns. This offline study evaluates whether repeated camera bearings and independent forward Doppler measurements can localize some of them. The running city driver and its rider display use the previously recorded sensor decisions.

The observer requires a current camera class of person with score at least 0.60. It considers boxes whose range the native tracker could not establish. A candidate 15–60 m ahead must have a plausible apparent body size and one or two exclusive laser returns. At least three uniquely projected Doppler echoes must support a laser surface within 0.29 m in camera depth and 0.50 m in the ground plane. The spread in radial velocity must be at most 0.65 m/s. The range must agree with an earlier independent observation of that image ID, at most 0.5 s old, after compensating for ego motion. Only the current observation supplies the reported position.

The implementation was frozen before scoring the complete corrected city tape. The observer SHA-256 was `ce536f0f34b0efc1e6877124c8204a5ec29e09c4ed0b2f72c5ce035b0bb5fa40`, and the independent scoring script SHA-256 was `1f6e5fd4abf5d30b9c106dcc5ea558bd84bf6a24d80aa9ea37d1deb76686eac9` (local source bytes). Actor IDs enter only after the observer has emitted positions. The evaluator assigns each position to a unique reference pedestrian within **1.5 m**. It also repeats the entire procedure with the radar azimuth shifted by **12°**. The shifted readings are a registration control. Synthetic controls cover close range, overlapping camera boxes, missing lidar, disagreeing radar depth or Doppler, stale timestamps and a discontinuous world position.

The first capture attempt informed development of the sparse observer. Its actor-radius reference was incomplete, and it is excluded from test results. The replacement capture repeats the early staged crossing. Other actors and later sections are stronger tests of generalization. Counts for distinct actors, any unmatched accepted reading and the shifted-radar control matter alongside coverage. A successful range estimate in this study is a research reading and does not retroactively add a 3D symbol to the recorded drive.
