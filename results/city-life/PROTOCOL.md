# City traffic, pace and early awareness

The reference drive is the 3,354-exposure Town03 recording from v0.4. The new drive uses the same coordinate system and simulation clock. We score driving decisions in matched fixtures separately from density on a route with additional people and traffic. More traffic can legitimately change the time needed to finish.

## Measurements fixed before the populated drive

- **Pace.** Use native speed, the policy's requested speed and the current posted limit on the same tick. Measure the 20 m from the first planted cone to the last and the 20 m immediately beyond it. Report median and minimum speed, elapsed time, stopping distance where relevant, contacts, and rule violations. A model that reaches the speed request only on a clear road still needs a blocked-lane test and a pedestrian test.
- **Activity near the car.** Count each registered road user within 50 m of the ego body once per frame. Classify a vehicle as moving at 1 m/s or faster and a pedestrian at 0.5 m/s or faster using displacement over the previous 0.5 simulated seconds. Require consecutive native frames. Ignore discontinuities and the first half-second of a run. Report how often a moving vehicle and a moving pedestrian are nearby simultaneously, how often two or more of each are nearby and the peak simultaneous counts. These are measurements of the *world*. Occluded actors still count here.
- **Sensor and screen.** Report the first camera evidence and first correctly positioned 3D drawing separately. A pixel location alone supplies a bearing. Retain the camera box when the scene lacks depth for a reliable 3D symbol. Compare nearby and distant cases, quantify missed observations and false detections, and retain unchanged camera footage. Sun et al. break down Waymo dataset performance at 0–30, 30–50 and above 50 m. These ranges help reveal where coverage falls, without assuming a human or commercial-system baseline. [Sun et al., CVPR 2020](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Sun_Scalability_in_Perception_for_Autonomous_Driving_Waymo_Open_Dataset_CVPR_2020_paper.html)
- **Physical evidence.** CARLA actor positions, IDs and scenario events may place traffic or grade a test. Only sensor evidence may introduce a road user or cone to the rider's perception display. The chase camera independently shows what happened in the simulator. [CARLA sensor reference](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/latest/ref_sensors/)
