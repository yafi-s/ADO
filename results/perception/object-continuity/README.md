# Rider object continuity on the v0.3 recordings

On the original 3,173-frame High city drive, the revised visible display matches **1,941 of 2,883 nearby scenario actor-frames**, up from 1,864. With registered parked vehicles included, matches rise from **2,273 to 2,350 of 4,198** and unmatched symbols fall from **2,025 to 1,722**. Both displays receive identical raw tracks, camera boxes, lidar, radar and ego poses. These are paired development results on three recordings. [Machine-readable results and individual case frames](same-tape.json)

The spatial memory alone improves from 1,841 to 1,902 scenario matches and from 2,250 to 2,311 with parked vehicles. Both versions match 409 parked actor-frames. The separate person/bicycle association contributes the remaining 23 and 39 matches. The scorer matches a symbol to one compatible reference within 3 m and reports actor coverage within 35 m. Occluded actors count in that denominator. An unmatched symbol can represent an unregistered object, a duplicate, a classification error, or a position error. [Original audit and native camera panels](../production/README.md)

The first pedestrian reaches half display opacity at 2.2 s, previously 3.4 s. The delayed crossing reaches it at 278.8 s, previously 279.3 s. A camera box alone still has no spatial position.
- **Cyclist passing on the left.** The front camera classifies the overlapping rider and bicycle. The same ranged person stays continuous through the left-camera handoff. The final association is two seconds old and the most recent wheel observation is still from the front. The original tracker supplies zero independently ranged bicycles. It remains the source of position and the display ends the association on an identity break, excessive depth age, conflicting bicycle range, separation or timeout.
- **Hedge and house frontage.** Source tracks 23 and 24 shared a weak car box. The assigned lidar surface spanned nearly 9 m. - **Residential frontage.** Tracks 50 and 55 yielded 57 unmatched truck symbol-ticks. The weak, single-view class now expires promptly after depth support ends. Seventeen truck ticks remain, ten current/recent and seven remembered. All seven native measurements remain available.
- **Roadside shelter and barrels.** A low-scoring rear-right car box had three lidar hits at the edge of a previously ranged structure over 3 m high. The revision keeps the surface unclassified. The original 37 car ticks (three current/recent and 34 remembered) fall to zero. The revision shows 27 unclassified ticks and all eight native ranging observations remain available. ## Motion, sector coverage and limits

The display fits a moving vehicle's world-space velocity from successive measured centres when its camera class and the direction of travel are well supported. A fast vehicle can return to a stopped position behind its predicted path when lidar and ego-motion-corrected Doppler support that stop. The city lead now matches 176 nearby ticks, previously 140. The stopped van and bus retain full coverage and zero duplicate frames. On the oncoming car, duplicate frames fall from 21 to 13 and the worst same-ID displacement error from 0.902 m to 0.561 m, with coverage falling by one tick to 110.

Core sector coverage on High changes from 961 to 993 of 1,447 in front, stays at 135 of 253 on the left, rises from 197 to 217 of 310 on the right, and from 548 to 557 of 873 behind. Across the entire scenario the display still has two direct ID switches and two reacquisitions. Among spatial matches, median position error changes from 0.231 to 0.247 m and P95 from 1.354 to 1.394 m. The city lead's P95 same-ID displacement error rises from 0.096 to 0.260 m over its expanded matched path. Across all nearby wheeled symbols, the largest per-tick extent change stays at 0.344 m and the largest position step rises from 1.005 to 1.014 m. A remembered position and a recent class can both be wrong, particularly behind the vehicle.

On the separate 116-frame clear pass and 183-frame yielding pass, both display versions match 115/116 and 264/265 nearby actor-frames, respectively. Their unmatched counts stay at six and five. Both moving leads and the adjacent vehicle retain zero duplicates. In the yielding pass the adjacent car's median position error improves from 0.653 to 0.458 m while P95 stays at 2.466 m. Eleven world-space steps now exceed 1 m per 0.1 s on that recording, previously zero. Its worst same-ID step error after subtracting actual actor motion stays at 0.564 m. These recordings do not establish general performance on other roads, weather, camera rigs or barrier classes.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run the comparison with Node and Git. It reads the original packed recordings, town geometry, parked-vehicle reference and v0.3 display code from release commit `a7a00120ff6e3f2e266db7de1880e31c38289553`. It runs the current and release display on each complete tape. Simulator actor records are available to the scorer only after the rider display has emitted its symbols. The output records every tape digest, display-module digest, optical-association digest and scoring-module digest. The current working tree must match those digests to reproduce the exact numbers.

```powershell
node tools/compare_rider_object_replays.mjs out/object-continuity.json
node --test tests/test_object_memory.mjs tests/test_rider_association.mjs tests/test_audit_city_perception.mjs
```
