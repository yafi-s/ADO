These are development records from the 1.42 km city course. The same road geometry is used during city-specific training and filming. Score the Town01 held-out study separately.

The dedicated city instructor uses privileged simulator clearance only for generating labels. With a four-metre standstill buffer and a nominal deceleration of 1.8 m/s², its requested speed is bounded by the available stopping distance. New city policies fit that speed response using measured RGB, lidar, radar, navigation and ego state. The city controller never receives the instructor's clearance, actor roles or event names. The instructor uses the elementary constant-deceleration stopping-distance relation. Longer-range interactive car-following models are discussed in Treiber et al.
