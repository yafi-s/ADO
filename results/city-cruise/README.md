# Town03 urban cruise development drives

The seed 211 learned drive completed 1,415.2 of 1,415.4 m in 316.6 simulated seconds, handled all ten encounters and traversed all eleven junctions. It registered zero contacts, red-light violations, yield violations or frames moving within one metre of a reference hazard. The scorekeeper observed stops for all three crossing pedestrians, the red-light queue, the stopped lead and the unprotected left. It observed the bicycle, work zone, bus and braking lead encounters clear in motion.

On clear straight segments with a native speed limit permitting at least 35 km/h, the car achieved a median of **35.01 km/h**, a 90th percentile of **36.48 km/h** and a peak of **36.61 km/h**. Its speed reached 35 km/h on 76 of 145 qualifying frames, including a continuous 4.2 seconds between 458.8 and 499.3 m. Across all clear straights, including 30 km/h sections, its median was **29.20 km/h**. Within the three turning junctions its median was **12.61 km/h**. The controller requested speeds at or below CARLA’s native posted limit on all 3,166 frames. No frame exceeded that limit by more than 1 km/h in measured vehicle speed.

The archived public v0.2 drive had a median of **17.79 km/h** across all clear straights, a whole-drive peak of **18.66 km/h** and a turning-junction median of **12.62 km/h** on the same nominal itinerary and seed. It took 354.3 simulated seconds. The archived drive used High rendering quality and its original planned bus pass. The new development drive used Low quality and the first tactical chooser. Speed differences describe the observed drives. They do not isolate the contribution of an individual change.

## Measurement and evidence

The speed analysis uses the velocity already measured by CARLA every 0.1 s. A clear-straight frame has no active encounter, at least 35 m of scorer reference clearance, and a station outside the span from 25 m before each junction to 8 m after its exit. The “permissive” subset additionally requires a measured posted limit of at least 35 km/h. This geometric definition precedes the seed 211 result and never uses achieved speed to select the subset. All driving metrics can be recomputed from the aligned trace, observed speed limits and junction stations. The archived v0.2 telemetry omitted posted limits, so its comparison uses all clear straights.

The driver uses 40 sensor and navigation features. The simulator’s speed-limit reading separately caps the speed request. The seed 211 policy-validation run used the original three driving cameras and forward radar with 360-degree LiDAR. The experiment enabled tactical lane selection. On the bus approach the provisional tactical chooser committed at 816.8 m and contributed 139 passing frames. The three-camera pilot predates final tactical and surround integration. Its result applies to the configuration recorded here.

The 4,707-byte provisional tactical checkpoint was reconstructed by repeating its original deterministic fit.
