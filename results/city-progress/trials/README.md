Each arm drives its own CARLA 1.0 simulation. The Market fixtures use the same start positions, ClearNoon weather, High rendering, 0.1 s step and 0.5 s pedestrian delay. The test moves the ego to its initial waypoint before scoring begins. The controller then chooses the steering and pedals on every tick. "Restarted" in these short drive summaries means the evaluator stopped at the designated end station. Check `target_pass`, the measured distance and the event exposure to read the result.

The two through-junction windows use seed 621 and the stopped-bus pair uses seed 622. Other Market controls use 623–626. The bus approach, occupied interval and return to speed have separate station windows. The bus event's station marks the beginning of its lane-change approach. The actual body is farther along the road. The controlled crossing starts with a native walker 1.6 m beyond the bus nose. The walker starts moving when the ego bumper reaches an 8 m approach distance. A ray from the ego camera to the walker tests occlusion against the bus's actual bounding box. The scorer separately records the pedestrian's entry into the passing lane, clearance and the ego bumper's arrival. The controller only receives its normal observations.

The road 67 fixtures occupy a different boulevard segment. One car drives slowly in the original lane while a second car initially occupies the passing lane. Both the RGB-plus-range and range-only configurations record every gap decision and issued pedal command. A pass requires sustained waiting, zero positive throttle or released hold before commitment, a physical gap to the other car and a completed overtake. The no-lead and departing-lead cases test for unnecessary passing and failure to resume following. The movable work-zone board on another road 67 segment has its own physical overlap and bumper-gap checks.

Front footage, frame IDs, simulator traces, model feature arrays and independent body references accompany the Market cases. In the comparison movie each side starts on its first sample at the named station. The film plays both cameras at native speed on the same elapsed clock. A side that exits first holds its last exposure and displays its exit time. The grade and film use the original ten samples per simulated second.
