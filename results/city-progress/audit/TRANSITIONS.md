# Abrupt target reductions

Supplement registered before the full candidate recording. On baseline and candidate traces count every single 0.1 s reduction of requested speed by at least 5 and at least 8 km/h. Save a location for every qualifying sample. For the candidate separately count a prior extension release (request at least 1 km/h above the recorded inherited request) immediately returning to within 0.1 km/h of the new inherited target. Include the guard name on both sides and its physical evidence. Combine successive drops less than 1 s apart into episodes for counting, while retaining the individual drops.

Report the minimum 0.5 s mean acceleration in the 1.5 s after the switch, the subsequent recovery to the preceding achieved speed or a sustained 10 s endpoint, and under-15 km/h samples in the next 5 s. Deduplicate overlapping attribution windows across episodes. This is temporal association, not proof that the switch caused every subsequent low-speed frame. Report the full-drive share of slow time as well as physical path occupancy and pedestrian proximity per episode. Strong braking, clusters in areas without a physically recorded conflict, and recurrent low-speed exposure are reasons to investigate the native film and sensor features at that station.

The lateral-comfort check was added before the full candidate results were sealed. Use six measured exposures spanning 0.5 s to compute absolute unwrapped ego heading change in radians per second times the trapezoidal mean native speed in m/s. Include every complete within-junction window, including stops, and calculate p95 and peak from the pooled exposures. Report all junctions, individual junctions including J1932, and all three actual turns separately. This is a kinematic body-heading estimate. Tire slip, roll, suspension and acceleration at the seat were not measured.
