# City Life progress audit protocol

Frozen before examining candidate outcomes. Evaluate the first full candidate on the same Town03 Market geometry, seed 212, City Life population, 0.5 s crossing delay and fixed 0.1 s step. Identify any mismatch, failed run, altered traffic timing, missing camera/reference or partial distance. Compare native simulation time and station. Render settings, checkpoint and sensor profile are reported for each run. Time savings alone cannot pass the audit.

- Publish every consecutive 25 m bin from spawn to destination, plus every native junction interior individually. Keep standstills and the first/last samples. Show achieved and requested median, lower decile, elapsed time, seconds below 5/10/15/20 km/h, moving coasting with both pedals below 0.05, positive speed request tracking error and posted limit. Use one fixed address for every window independent of candidate behavior. Never discard a whole intersection based on one busy exposure.
- Also retain original matched boulevard [420.16, 501.65) m, shared approach [734, 770) m, bus approach [770, 800), alongside [800, 825), return [825, 855) and postpass [855, 890) m. Report every straight and turn separately. Fixed [150, 250), [420, 500), [600, 645), [855, 890), [1080, 1180), [1365, 1415) m are additional between-junction sections. These are geography comparisons. Do not label them clear without actor and phase evidence.
- At each station and exposure examine ground-truth forward path occupancy against the native mapped/planned route or measured ego trajectory, including moving actors, parked map vehicles and adjacent side-road users. Report vehicle-body occupancy within 25 m of the nose and separate vulnerable road users within 1.5 m of the swept body; neither screening flag proves imminent collision. Outside both, call the sample “no nearby path actor recorded.” Missing references, cross traffic outside the ribbon, offscreen road users, sightlines and prop exposures remain uncertainty. Evaluate per-frame flags, active staged hazards, source video, occluded scenes, curves and signal states separately. Use scorer-only data for evaluation.
- For every native marked release, evaluate 0–1, 1–3 and 3–5 s intervals and first subsequent 5/10/15/20 km/h within 10 s, even if a new encounter follows. Report the station, target and speed at release, and gains over the next 5 s. Distinguish passing, scenario “cleared”, native departure/restart, and genuinely available space. Do not demand acceleration toward an occupied corridor.
- Count and localize contiguous stretches >=2 s under 15 km/h, report their duration and the slowest station bins. Check actual vs requested speeds, actuator behavior, differences among turns, straight intersections and open roads. Look for delayed recovery, lingering low targets, excessive coasting, speed overshoots and raw/0.5 s acceleration/deceleration. Retain raw ego speed and native requested speed. Evaluate speed excess above 40 km/h and posted+1 km/h, jerk from consecutive 0.5 s average accelerations separated by 0.5 s and duration of sustained braking harder than 3 m/s².
- Require route completion, all eleven staged encounters exposed/passed/handled, all eleven junctions, zero contacts, missed yields and red-line violations. Recheck event-specific closest gaps, body footprints for every available vehicle/pedestrian (including bus and unrelated road users), overlap and close (<0.5, <1 m) encounters, exposure at speed, plus minimum cone/barrier gaps. Positive clearance is not proof a close pass is prudent. List regressions and unmatched conditions separately. Small or absent speed change on any window stays visible even when total time improves.
- Diagnostic camera samples: baseline at the midpoint sample of every consecutive 25 m segment and at native release +0, +2 and +5 s for each event. Use the original film frame/inspector mapping. Visual claims must cite a view and frame. Candidate camera comparisons use the same stations and release offsets where retained. Surface partial observability rather than claiming collision-free ground truth between 10 Hz samples.
